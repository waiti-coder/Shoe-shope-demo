Questions?

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery and exchanges.

How do I pay?

Pay via Lipa na M-Pesa using the Till/Paybill details shown at checkout and send us the M-Pesa confirmation message on WhatsApp. In Nairobi you can also pay cash on delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Nairobi orders placed before 2pm are usually delivered the same day. Nearby counties take 1–2 days and the rest of Kenya 2–4 days, via courier or matatu parcel service.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is calculated at checkout based on your county.

What if the shoes don't fit?

We offer free size exchanges within 7 days of delivery, as long as the shoes are unworn and in their original box. Just WhatsApp us.

Can I pick up from your shop?

Yes. Choose "Pick up" at checkout and we'll let you know when your order is ready.
