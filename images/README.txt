PRODUCT PHOTOS
==============

Save a photo of each shoe in this folder, named after the product's "slug"
from js/products.js. It will show up automatically on the home page showcase,
shop grid, product view, cart and wishlist.

  nairobi-runner.jpg          kileleshwa-stiletto.jpg
  westlands-court-low.jpg     karen-nude-pump.jpg
  kilimani-leather-loafer.jpg midnight-runner.jpg
  lamu-leather-sandal.jpg     tsavo-chelsea-boot.jpg
  maasai-mara-trail-boot.jpg  ... and so on (.jpg, .png or .webp)

Tips for great photos (a phone is fine):
  - Shoot in daylight near a window, on a plain white, beige or light wall/sheet.
  - Side view of one shoe, toe pointing right, filling most of the frame.
  - Landscape (wider than tall), about 1200 x 900 pixels.
  - Compress before uploading (e.g. squoosh.app) - aim for under 200 KB each.

Products without a photo keep showing the built-in illustration.
